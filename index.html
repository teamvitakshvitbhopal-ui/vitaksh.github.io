Who We Are

Team VITAKSH is a student-led aerospace research team committed to advancing space technology through hands-on engineering projects. Our mission is to inspire innovation by designing and developing satellite systems, high-altitude balloon payloads, avionics, communication systems, and ground station technologies.


The team brings together students from various engineering disciplines who collaborate to solve complex engineering challenges while contributing to India's growing space ecosystem.

Vision

To become a nationally recognized student aerospace research team that empowers future engineers through innovation, research, and space technology.

Mission

  • Design and develop reliable aerospace systems.
  • Promote research and innovation among students.
  • Build industry-ready engineering skills through practical projects.
  • Represent the university in national and international level competitions.
  • Contribute towards India's expanding space ecosystem.

Achievements

  • We successfully launched the HABSAT mission.
  • We designed an indigenous atmospheric payload.
  • We developed an in-house telemetry and communication system.
  • We participated in national aerospace competitions.
  • We built a multidisciplinary engineering team.
