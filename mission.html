Projects & Research

High Altitude Balloon Satellite (HABSAT)

The HABSAT mission was developed to conduct atmospheric profiling and near-space experimentation using a high-altitude balloon platform.

Mission Objectives

  • Measure atmospheric parameters
  • Capture high-altitude imagery
  • Demonstrate real-time telemetry
  • Validate onboard avionics
  • Test payload recovery systems

Payload Features

  • Environmental Sensors
  • GPS Tracking & IMU
  • ESP32-based distributed computing
  • Raspberry Pi onboard processing
  • LoRa Communication & Camera System

Current Research Domains

  • Satellite Systems (CubeSat, HABSAT)
  • Communication & Ground Station
  • Avionics & Embedded Systems
  • Atmospheric Science & Flight Software
  • Artificial Intelligence & Autonomous Systems

Our Journey

VITAKSH 1.0

The foundation of Team VITAKSH was laid with a vision to introduce students to practical aerospace engineering and collaborative innovation.

VITAKSH 2.0

We successfully designed, built, and launched a High Altitude Balloon Satellite (HABSAT) carrying scientific payloads for atmospheric data collection and near-space experimentation.

VITAKSH 3.0

We are excited to begin our next major projects: the development of a Ground Station and a CanSat. These projects will strengthen our expertise in satellite communication and mission operations.
